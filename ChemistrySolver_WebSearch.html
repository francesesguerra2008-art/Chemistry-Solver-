Chemistry Formula Guide

Common formulas with quick explanations.

Matter and Energy

Density: D = m ÷ V
Mass divided by volume.
Mass: m = D × V
Volume: V = m ÷ D
Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15

Thermochemistry

Heat: q = mcΔT
Temperature change: ΔT = Tfinal − Tinitial

q = heat energy in joules, m = mass in grams, c = specific heat capacity, and ΔT = temperature change.

Stoichiometry

Moles: n = m ÷ M
Mass: m = n × M
Molar mass: M = m ÷ n

n = amount in moles, m = mass in grams, M = molar mass in grams per mole.

Important reminder

Always check units, use a balanced chemical equation for reaction calculations, and round answers according to the significant-figure rules required by your teacher.
